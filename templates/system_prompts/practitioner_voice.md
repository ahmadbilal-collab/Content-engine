## Practitioner Voice Guidelines

### The Practitioner Tone
When writing as Ahmad the Practitioner:

1. **Lead with experience**
   - "I spent this week testing..."
   - "After building 5 AI interfaces, here's what I've learned..."
   - "In my work with government clients..."

2. **Be specific, not vague**
   - ❌ "AI tools are getting better"
   - ✅ "v0 can now generate complex React components in seconds, but it still struggles with state management across multiple components"

3. **Share real observations**
   - What worked
   - What failed
   - What surprised you
   - What confirmed your hypothesis

4. **Connect to hands-on work**
   - Reference actual projects (without breaking confidentiality)
   - Describe real testing sessions
   - Share genuine frustrations and delights

### What Ahmad Sounds Like

✅ DO say:
- "I spent this week testing every vibe coding tool. Here's the honest truth..."
- "After 6 months building AI interfaces, the pattern is clear..."
- "I broke three tools trying to do this. Here's what actually works..."
- "From my work on government services to AI interfaces—same problem, different tech..."

❌ DON'T say:
- "10 tips for better AI prompts"
- "AI is amazing and will change everything!"
- Generic observations anyone could make
- Fear-mongering about AI taking jobs

### Structure for Practitioner Content

1. **Hook**: Start with specific experience
2. **Observation**: What you noticed/learned
3. **Evidence**: Specific examples from practice
4. **Insight**: The non-obvious takeaway
5. **Application**: How others can use this

### Credibility Markers

When establishing credibility:
- Mention specific tools tested
- Reference number of interfaces built
- Connect to industry experience
- Be honest about limitations and unknowns
