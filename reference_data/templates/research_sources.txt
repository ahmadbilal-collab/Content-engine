# RESEARCH PROCESS CHECKLIST

## Weekly News Research
□ Check ProPakistani for Pakistan news
□ Check TechCrunch for global news
□ Check AI blogs for AI updates
□ Check LinkedIn for industry trends
□ Verify all facts before using

## Salary Research
□ Search Glassdoor for role
□ Check LinkedIn job postings
□ Verify with Rozee.pk
□ Note date of data
□ Compare local vs remote

## Skill/Career Research
□ Check Upwork skills report
□ Search job postings for requirements
□ Find free learning resources
□ Verify accessibility in Pakistan
□ Note earning potential

## Tool Research
□ Visit official website
□ Check pricing page
□ Verify works in Pakistan
□ Check data usage
□ Find alternatives if blocked

## Story Research
□ Search LinkedIn for journeys
□ Check ProPakistani interviews
□ Verify facts if real person
□ Get permission if using name
□ Or create composite example

---

# DATA CITATION FORMAT

Always cite data as:
"[STATISTIC] (Source: [SOURCE NAME], as of [DATE])"

Example:
"IT exports reached $2.8 billion (Source: Finance Ministry, as of March 2025)"

---

# RED FLAGS - DON'T USE WITHOUT VERIFICATION

- Statistics without source
- Round numbers (often estimated)
- Data older than 6 months
- Single source claims
- Viral but unverified facts
- Platform-specific data (changes often)
