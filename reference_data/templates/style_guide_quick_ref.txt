# Bharte Chalo - Style Guide Quick Reference

## LANGUAGE MIX FORMULA
60% English + 40% Roman Urdu = Natural Pakistani Speech

## WHEN TO USE URDU
✓ Greetings: Assalam-u-Alaikum, Khush aamdeed
✓ Emotions: Dil se baat, Aap akele nahi hain
✓ Encouragement: Aap kar sakte hain, Himmat na harein
✓ Emphasis: Yeh bohat zaroori hai, Dhyan se suniye
✓ Transitions: Ab baat karte hain, Chalein aagey
✓ Closing: Allah Hafiz, Phir milenge

## WHEN TO USE ENGLISH
✓ Technical terms (with Urdu explanation)
✓ Company/platform names
✓ Statistics and numbers
✓ Job titles and skill names
✓ Global concepts

## TONE GUIDE
✓ Conversational like talking to a smart friend
✓ Warm and welcoming energy
✓ Enthusiastic but not hyper
✓ Hopeful but realistic
✓ Respectful of listener intelligence

## BAD vs GOOD EXAMPLES

BAD: "Artificial Intelligence is defined as..."
GOOD: "AI basically yeh hai ke computer ko insaan ki tarah sochna sikha dein"

BAD: "You should learn programming"
GOOD: "Programming seekhna chahiye - agar aapke paas laptop hai toh aaj hi shuru karo"

BAD: "The job market is competitive"
GOOD: "Market tough hai - lekin agar skill sahi ho toh opportunity bhi milti hai"

## PAKISTANI CONTEXT RULES
✓ Every tool → "Works in Pakistan? Data usage?"
✓ Every salary → Pakistan + remote/dollar comparison
✓ Every resource → Free options first
✓ Every tip → Consider load shedding, slow internet
✓ Every story → Connect to Pakistani reality

## CITIES TO MENTION (Not just Karachi/Lahore)
Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan,
Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot, Rawalpindi, Hyderabad

## SEGMENT ENERGY GUIDE
Opening: HIGH energy, welcoming
Tech Samjho: CLEAR, educational
Tips: PRACTICAL, helpful
Skill/Career: INFORMATIVE, encouraging
Kahani: STORYTELLING, emotional
Game: FUN, engaging
News: INFORMATIVE, quick
Q&A: HELPFUL, personal
Global/Jobs: PRACTICAL, hopeful
Closing: WARM, motivating

## SIGNATURE PHRASES
Show Name: "Bharte Chalo!"
Tagline: "Sochein Behtar, Faisla Karein Smarter, Aagey Barhein"
Closing: "Allah Hafiz Pakistan!"
Encouragement: "Aap zaroor kar sakte hain!"
Call to Action: "Aaj hi shuru karein!"

## TIMING DISCIPLINE
Each segment has allocated time - stick to it:
- Opening: 8-10 min
- Tech Samjho: 10 min
- Tips: 8 min
- Skill/Career: 10 min
- Kahani: 10 min
- Game: 5 min
- News: 12 min
- Q&A: 10 min
- Global/Jobs: 7 min
- Closing: 8-10 min
TOTAL: ~90 minutes

## ACTIONABLE CONTENT RULE
Every segment MUST end with:
- What listener should DO
- Specific next step
- Resource or link if applicable

## DATA CITATION RULE
Every statistic MUST have:
- Source name
- Date (as of when)
- Pakistan relevance

## QUALITY CHECKLIST (Quick)
□ Bilingual balance feels natural?
□ Every segment has Pakistan context?
□ Actionable takeaways in each segment?
□ Technical terms explained simply?
□ Energy appropriate for segment?
□ Statistics cited with sources?
□ Free options mentioned first?
