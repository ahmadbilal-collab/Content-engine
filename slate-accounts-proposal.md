## My Approach: "Trustworthy + Fun" in B2B

Here's how I think about this balance:

**Trust comes from:**
- Clear visual hierarchy that respects the reader's time
- Consistent patterns that feel predictable (in a good way)
- Professional typography and generous white space
- Social proof and credibility signals placed strategically
- Accessibility and attention to detail

**Fun comes from:**
- Strategic use of your bold image treatment (sounds like you have great art direction already)
- Micro-interactions and subtle motion (in handoff notes for dev)
- Unexpected but appropriate accent color moments
- Copy-led design—letting your personality shine through layout
- Breaking the grid occasionally, with purpose

**The sweet spot:** Design that feels like talking to the smartest, most capable accountant you've ever met—who also happens to have a great sense of humor and genuinely cares about your business.

I won't give you "corporate beige" or safe, forgettable design. But I also won't give you gimmicks that undermine credibility. This is about confidence, not caution.
