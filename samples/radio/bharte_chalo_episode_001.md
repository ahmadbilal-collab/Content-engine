## 2.5 Common Mistakes (Yeh Galtiyan Mat Karein)
*Duration: 3 minutes*

**HOST:**

"Ab important baat - common mistakes jo log karte hain AI use karte waqt:

**MISTAKE 1: Vague Prompts**
- GALAT: 'Article likho'
- SAHI: 'Mujhe 800 words ka article chahiye Pakistani freelancers ke liye, topic: Upwork pe pehla client kaise milega, tone: friendly aur practical'

**MISTAKE 2: AI pe Blindly Trust Karna**
- AI kabhi kabhi galat information deta hai
- HAMESHA verify karein
- Especially facts aur figures double check karein

**MISTAKE 3: Copy-Paste Seedha**
- AI ka output seedha use mat karein
- Apna touch daalein
- Edit karein, personalize karein

**MISTAKE 4: Sirf Ek Tool pe Depend**
- Multiple tools try karein
- Different tools different cheezon mein ache hain
- ChatGPT writing mein, Claude coding mein, Gemini research mein

**MISTAKE 5: Practice Na Karna**
- Sirf sun ke nahi hoga
- Daily use karein
- Experiment karein
- Galtiyan karein, seekhein

**Pakistani specific mistake:**
Internet slow hone pe give up kar dena. Solution: Prompts offline likhein, jab net aaye tab paste karein. Mobile data bhi use kar sakte hain - ChatGPT app light hai."
